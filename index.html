OUR STORY

Rooted in tradition.
Made for today.

For 15 years, we've been part of the world of khaau — little treats that bring people together. Usha Nutrifoods is our new chapter, offering nine varieties of traditional laddoos.

Made at home, by hand, with pure ghee, good jaggery and quality ingredients. No refined sugar, added colours or preservatives.
