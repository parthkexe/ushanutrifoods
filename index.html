A LITTLE ABOUT US

15 years of experience.
A new beginning.

We have been in the khaau business for 15 years. Over the years, we have learnt what goes into making good traditional food and how important it is to use the right ingredients.

Usha Nutrifoods is our new venture. We currently make nine varieties of laddoos. Each one is prepared at home, by hand, using pure ghee, jaggery and carefully selected ingredients.

We have more varieties in the works and look forward to sharing them with you.
